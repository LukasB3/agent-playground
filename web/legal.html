Rules for using the playground

You describe a small web app, software builds it automatically, and the result is published on a public page. By sending a request you agree to the following.

  • Do not try to overload or circumvent the service's limits.
  • Generated apps come without any warranty. They are demos, may contain mistakes and may be removed at any time without notice.

Generated apps and how to report one

The apps are written by an AI model from visitors' requests and published at lukasb3.github.io/agent-playground-apps without prior review by a person. Each one is checked automatically, cannot contact other servers, and carries a badge marking it as AI-generated. They do not reflect my views. Each app stays online for 30 days and is then removed.

If an app infringes your rights or is unlawful, email me its address at bossert.dev@gmail.com. I remove such apps without delay once I learn of them.

The captcha

To keep automated abuse out, the form loads Turnstile from Cloudflare, Inc. (USA). Turnstile runs a check in your browser and transmits your IP address and technical browser information to Cloudflare, which returns a result that my server verifies. This is necessary to protect the service and its budget (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Sending a request

When you send a request, my server (rented from Hetzner Online GmbH, located in Falkenstein, Germany) receives the text and your IP address. It stores the text, the time, the progress of the job and a keyed hash of your IP address, which lets it enforce the limit per visitor without keeping the address itself. These records are deleted after 30 days. The server keeps no access log with IP addresses.

The text of your request is sent to Anthropic, PBC (USA) through its API, because its AI model writes the app. Anthropic does not receive your IP address from me.

I process this data to provide the service you asked for and to protect it against abuse (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR).

Apps are removed automatically after 30 days. You can ask me to remove an app you requested earlier than that by emailing me its address.
